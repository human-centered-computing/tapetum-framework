\subsection{Dataset-Adaptive Hyperparameter Selection and Training Configuration}
\label{subsec:implementation_details}

Hyperparameter optimization is performed independently for LOL-v1, LOL-v2
Real-Captured, LOL-v2 Synthetic, and UHD-LL down4. Rather than applying a
single configuration to all datasets, we construct a dataset-specific search
space from measurable properties of each training partition. The analyzed
properties include target-to-input luminance gain, dark-pixel coverage,
dark-region high-frequency activity, normalized color shift, target chroma,
detail gain, edge strength, and spatial variation in darkness. These
measurements are used only to activate relevant parameter groups and constrain
their candidate domains; they do not directly determine the final
hyperparameter values. The activation thresholds are prespecified engineering
rules rather than learned decision boundaries.

All data partitions are fixed before optimization. Using split seed 42, the
485 training pairs of LOL-v1 are divided into 437 optimization and 48
validation pairs, the 689 LOL-v2 Real-Captured pairs into 620 and 69 pairs,
and the 900 LOL-v2 Synthetic pairs into 810 and 90 pairs, respectively. For
UHD-LL down4, 2,000 training pairs are divided into 1,884 optimization and
116 validation pairs. The UHD-LL split additionally keeps related numeric
filename blocks in the same partition to reduce leakage between closely
indexed images. Dataset statistics used to construct the search spaces are
computed exclusively from the optimization partition, using a deterministic
sample of at most 256 paired images. Validation targets are reserved for
candidate comparison, while the official test partitions are excluded from
both search-space construction and hyperparameter selection.

Optimization is organized into four progressive stages. The optimizer stage
searches the initial learning rate, the minimum-to-initial learning-rate ratio,
and the gradient-clipping norm. The illumination stage searches
$\lambda_{\max}$. The fidelity stage searches the SSIM and LPIPS loss weights,
and the detail stage searches the activated color, chroma, edge, dark-region
noise, and tapetum-attention weights. Parameters whose dataset-level activation
criteria are not satisfied remain fixed at their source-profile values. The
source-profile value of every optimized parameter is also retained within its
candidate domain, and the complete source profile is included as a control
configuration.

The staged searches use 10, 10, 12, and 14 target trials for the optimizer,
illumination, fidelity, and detail stages, respectively. For LOL-v1 and LOL-v2
Real-Captured, the corresponding cumulative epoch targets are 25, 30, 35, and
40 epochs. LOL-v2 Synthetic uses cumulative targets of 20, 25, 30, and 35
epochs. Because UHD-LL down4 contains substantially more optimization pairs,
its cumulative targets are reduced to 8, 10, 12, and 15 epochs to provide a
comparable number of optimizer updates. At each stage, the configurations of
the next stage branch from the selected configuration's final checkpoint.
Model weights, Adam state, scheduler state, and the completed-epoch index are
preserved during this progressive continuation, whereas the best-metric
history is reset at each stage boundary so that newly introduced
hyperparameters are evaluated only on epochs produced under the updated
configuration.

Following the staged search, the exact source-profile baseline and the two
leading distinct HPO configurations are independently trained from scratch
with seeds $\{42,123,3407\}$ for up to 120 epochs. These runs do not inherit
the staged-search checkpoints, thereby preserving independence across seeds.
The final dataset-specific configuration is selected by applying
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:hpo-psnr-tolerance}--\eqref{eq:hpo-lpips-selection} to the
three-seed mean validation metrics. As reported in
Table~\ref{tab:retinextapetum-hpo-three-seed}, the selected configurations
obtain $21.907\pm0.077$~dB PSNR, $0.8652\pm0.0022$ SSIM, and
$0.1256\pm0.0055$ LPIPS on LOL-v1; $21.576\pm0.037$~dB,
$0.8317\pm0.0004$, and $0.1615\pm0.0059$ on LOL-v2 Real-Captured;
and $25.173\pm0.045$~dB, $0.9357\pm0.0014$, and
$0.0554\pm0.0011$ on LOL-v2 Synthetic. For UHD-LL down4, the
source-profile baseline remains the selected configuration, achieving
$27.035\pm0.178$~dB, $0.9263\pm0.0021$, and
$0.0670\pm0.0027$, respectively.

RetinexTapetum is implemented in PyTorch and contains 536,463 trainable
parameters. All experiments use a base channel width of 128, a batch size of
2, $256\times256$ training crops, and the Adam optimizer with
$\beta_1=0.9$ and $\beta_2=0.999$. The learning rate is reduced using cosine
annealing over a 120-epoch horizon. The parameters held constant across all
datasets are summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:retinextapetum-fixed-hyperparameters}, whereas the selected
dataset-specific learning rates, minimum learning rates, gradient-clipping
norms, amplification limits, and enhancement-loss weights are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:retinextapetum-hyperparameters}. Thus, each dataset is trained
with its own selected configuration rather than receiving parameters
transferred from another dataset.

Within every training run, the checkpoint with the highest validation PSNR is
retained. SSIM and LPIPS are monitored as complementary structural and
perceptual indicators but do not determine the within-run checkpoint epoch.
LPIPS is evaluated after resizing to 256 pixels during the preliminary staged
search and to 512 pixels during final multi-seed confirmation. The test LPIPS
values are obtained from the paper-metric evaluation pipeline and are
therefore not used for direct validation--test checkpoint comparisons.
Training diagnostics, validation performance, and independent test results for
the selected checkpoints are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:retinextapetum-train-test}. The official test partitions are
evaluated only after configuration and checkpoint selection. No test-time
hyperparameter tuning, post-hoc color restoration, or dataset-specific output
correction is applied.
